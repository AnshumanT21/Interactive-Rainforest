## STEP B: Bring each scene to life (video, 16:9, 8 seconds)

**How:** in Gemini, choose **Video** (Veo), upload the image named in each prompt, and paste the prompt. Download the video.

**The rules that matter most:**
- **The camera must not move.** My site moves the camera itself, so a video that pans or zooms will fight it. Every prompt says so; if a result still moves, regenerate it.
- **The bird stays in the same spot.** It moves its head and feathers, but it doesn't walk off or fly away.
- **Sound doesn't matter.** I'll handle sound separately.

**Make them in this order** (most important first), in case you run out of video credits:

1. **v12-waterfall.mp4** · upload `p12-waterfall-clean`

Then a small flock of colourful budgerigars (blue, green, yellow) flies across the top of the frame from left to right, flapping quickly. Photorealistic nature documentary footage. No people, no text.
